Cleaned Canva illustrations — 12 SVGs and PNG exports with transparent backgrounds.

Eight assets are fully vector SVGs, with the full-page white background removed.
Four assets (Poster.svg, Poster (8).svg, Poster (9).svg, Poster (11).svg) contain embedded raster PNG images; recovered transparency using their own embedded grayscale masks. They remain SVG containers for raster imagery, not true vector art.
Character whites, eyes, and black outlines were deliberately preserved.
